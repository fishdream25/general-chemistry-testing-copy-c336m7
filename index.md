# General Chemistry (testing copy)

A complete introductory general chemistry course spanning a two-semester sequence, from measurement and atomic structure through stoichiometry, gases, thermochemistry, quantum theory, periodic trends, bonding and molecular geometry, intermolecular forces, solutions, kinetics, equilibrium, acid-base and solubility equilibria, thermodynamics, electrochemistry, and nuclear chemistry. Each chapter includes a concept map, study guide, slide deck, assessment guide, and practice questions.
