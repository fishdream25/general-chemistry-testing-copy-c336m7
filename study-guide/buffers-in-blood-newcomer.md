# Buffers in Blood (newcomer)

Blood stays between pH 7.35 and 7.45 because of the bicarbonate buffer. The reaction is $\ce{CO2 + H2O <=> H2CO3}$ and the ratio follows $pH = pK_a + \log\frac{[HCO3^-]}{[H2CO3]}$ (newcomer).
